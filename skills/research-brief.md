# Skill: evidence-bound research brief

Produce a concise brief answering the user's question from available files and explicitly permitted sources.

1. Restate the decision/question in one internal sentence, but do not use a preamble in the answer.
2. Search for primary evidence first. Record source path/URL and the exact supporting passage.
3. Separate observed facts, calculations, and inference.
4. Reconcile conflicting sources by date, authority, and scope; do not silently choose one.
5. Cite every material claim with the source marker supplied by retrieval.
6. Include uncertainty, missing evidence, and what would change the conclusion.

Output: answer first; 3–7 findings; implications; sources; remaining unknowns. Never fabricate a citation or claim retrieval found text it did not return.
